# Handoff: OPAC-009 — Extreme rays of the Schur cone / cone of log-concavity

## Setup

Let $\mathcal{C}^k_N \subseteq \Lambda_N$ be the cone generated by all products $s_{\rho^1}s_{\rho^2}\cdots$ of Schur functions, homogeneous of total degree $N$, where each $\rho^i$ has at most $k$ parts. Write $\mathcal{SP}^k_N$ for the corresponding multisets $A$ of partitions and $s_A$ for the product. The problem is to identify the extreme rays.

For $k=1$ the $s_{(i)}=h_i$ are a basis, so all are extreme; for $k=\infty$ the LR rule makes all $s_\lambda$ extreme. The interesting case is $k=2$, where Jacobi–Trudi shows $\mathcal{C}^2_N$ is generated by $h_ih_j - h_{i+1}h_{j-1}$ ($i \ge j > 0$) and $h_i$; this is the **cone of log-concavity**.

Call a pair $\{\lambda,\mu\}$ *interlaced* if it satisfies one of

- (i) $\lambda=(\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2>0)$, $\mu=(\mu_1\ge\mu_2>0)$ with $\lambda_1>\mu_1\ge\lambda_2>\mu_2$;
- (ii) $\lambda=(\lambda_1>\lambda_2>0)$, $\mu=(\mu_1>0)$ with $\lambda_1\ge\mu_1\ge\lambda_2$;
- (iii) $\lambda=(\lambda_1>0)$, $\mu=(\mu_1>0)$;

and call $A$ *nested* if no pair in $A$ is interlaced.

**Theorem (White).** If $A$ is not nested then $s_A$ is not extreme. (Direct Jacobi–Trudi identities.)

**Theorem (White).** If $A$ is nested and all parts of all partitions in $A$ are distinct, then $s_A$ is extreme. (Farkas' Lemma plus the LR rule.)

**Conjecture (OPAC-009).** If $A$ is nested then $s_A$ is extreme.

OPAC-010 (describe the extreme vectors of $\mathcal{C}^k_N$ for $k\ge 3$) is the stretch goal; ignore it unless $k=2$ falls.

## Status — read this first

Gaetz, Meyer, Tam, Wimberley, Yao and Zhu give an alternate proof of White's distinct-parts theorem, establish several further infinite families of extreme rays, and **reduce White's conjecture to two simpler conjectures**. Start by reading that paper and taking up the two reduced conjectures directly; do not re-derive what is there. The obstruction is repeated parts, which reads as a technical degeneracy in the Farkas argument rather than a conceptual wall.

## Method

Extremality certificates here are separating linear functionals, and they are exactly computable. Expand $s_A = \sum_\mu c^A_\mu s_\mu$ in the Schur basis, so every generator is an explicit rational vector in $\Lambda_N$. Then $s_A$ is extreme iff there is a linear functional $\varphi$ on $\Lambda_N$ separating $s_A$ from the cone generated by the other generators. Suggested pipeline:

1. Enumerate nested $A \in \mathcal{SP}^2_N$ for $N$ up to roughly $12$–$14$.
2. Solve the separation LP in **exact** rational arithmetic to produce certificates $\varphi_A$.
3. Look for a closed form for $\varphi_A$ as a function of $A$ — in particular for nested $A$ with repeated parts, where White's construction breaks down.
4. Prove the required inequalities $\varphi_A(s_A) < \varphi_A(s_B)$ uniformly, using the LR rule and Jacobi–Trudi, for whatever family the closed form covers.

The LP is a lemma-generator only. Verifying extremality numerically for many $A$ is not the deliverable and should not consume the run.

## Success criterion

At least one proven infinite family of extreme rays not already in White or in Gaetz et al. — for example, all nested $A$ in which exactly one partition has a repeated part, or all nested $A$ consisting of two-part partitions only. Resolving either of the two reduced conjectures of Gaetz et al. would of course also count, as would the full conjecture.
